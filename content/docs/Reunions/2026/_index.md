# 119th Rinehart Family Reunion

<img src="/assets/Rinehart-Logo-from-Kent-354x176.jpg" style="float: right; margin-left: 20px;" alt="Rineharts">

### August 6 ‐ 9, 2026  Cove, Oregon

This year, it is our honor and privilege to extend you an invitation to the 119th Rinehart Family Reunion,
  which will be held in the very same valley as that first reunion so long ago back in 1903.
  Please join us for the Rinehart Reunion of a lifetime!

**Hosts:** Armin & Beth Reschke  
**Date:** Thursday, August 6 - Sunday, August 9  (Thursday afternoon - Sunday morning)  
**Place:** Ascension Camp & Conference Center 1104 Church St, Cove, OR 97824  
**Website:** [https://coveascensionschool.com](https://coveascensionschool.com)  

This facility is nestled at the foot of the Wallowa Mountains and is just 16 miles from La Grande, Oregon.  Make new memories and celebrate our long family legacy set in the idyllic Grande Ronde Valley.

**Friday** ‐ Enjoy genealogy classes, water activities, outdoor games or just explore.  
**Saturday** ‐ Tour the site of the old Rinehart Family mill and compound.  Explore our family heritage at the Summerville Cemetery.
